A new school year brings new opportunities to support the mental and physical well-being of our youth.

RISE UP is a two-day, Indigenous-led Mental Health, Prevention and Fitness Program designed specifically for First Nations youth.

Physical fitness and mental health are deeply connected. Movement can improve mood, reduce stress, build confidence and help young people develop healthy habits that support their overall well-being.

Carlin is grateful for every opportunity to share his personal journey and some of the lessons he learned through hockey, education and life. The goal is never to tell young people what they should become — it is to help them recognize the strength, ability and potential they already have.

**Strong Mind. Strong Body. Strong Community.**

Now accepting school and community bookings across Canada for the 2026–27 school year.
