A workshop that names and addresses lateral violence — harmful behaviour directed within a community rather than at the real source of harm — and gives youth tools to break the cycle.

Carlin helps participants recognize these patterns in themselves and their peer groups, and builds a path toward mutual support instead of internal conflict.
