An interactive workshop connecting physical health to mental wellness, helping youth build healthy habits, confidence, and a stronger sense of self.

Carlin draws on his own experience as a professional athlete to show how small, consistent habits — movement, sleep, nutrition, and mindset — build the foundation for long-term wellbeing.
