Booking the wrong speaker is an expensive mistake — not just in fees, but in a wasted opportunity to actually move your audience. Before you commit, run through these ten questions.

1. **What's their story, specifically?** Vague claims of "inspiring others" aren't enough. Ask what the speaker's personal story actually is and whether it's genuinely theirs.

2. **Have they spoken to an audience like mine before?** A speaker who's great with corporate executives may not land with a gym full of teenagers, and vice versa.

3. **How do they customize their presentation?** The best speakers ask about your event, audience, and goals before showing up — not after.

4. **What do they want the audience to walk away with?** If the answer is just "feeling inspired," push further. Good keynotes leave people with something they can actually use.

5. **Can they provide references?** Past clients — schools, companies, or organizations — can tell you things a sales page never will.

6. **What are their technical requirements?** Confirm AV needs, room setup, and timing well before the event, not the morning of.

7. **How do they handle a mixed or difficult audience?** Ask how they've adapted on the fly when a room wasn't responding the way they expected.

8. **Is there a follow-up component?** A single talk can spark something, but speakers who offer materials, workshops, or ongoing engagement tend to leave a longer-lasting impact.

9. **What's included in the fee?** Travel, accommodations, and prep time should be clear upfront to avoid surprises.

10. **Do they seem genuinely invested in your event, or is this just another date on their calendar?** This one you'll feel in the first conversation.
